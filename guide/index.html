Key Stats Explained

  • Words: Tokens separated by whitespace. Numbers and hyphenated words count as one word.
  • Characters (with spaces): Every character including spaces — used by Twitter, LinkedIn, SMS.
  • Characters (without spaces): Non-whitespace characters only — used in some word processors.
  • Sentences: Counts full stops, exclamation marks and question marks as sentence endings.
  • Unique words: Distinct words (case-insensitive). Useful for vocabulary richness checks.
  • Keyword density: Top N words by frequency. Useful for SEO content checks.

Text Cleaning Tools

  • Remove extra spaces: Collapses multiple spaces into one and trims leading/trailing whitespace.
  • Case conversion: UPPER CASE, lower case, Title Case, or Sentence case.
  • Remove line breaks: Joins all lines into one — useful for pasting into single-line fields.
  • Remove numbers / special characters: Strip digits or punctuation from text.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time = Word count ÷ 200 (average adult reading speed in wpm). Speaking time uses 130 wpm. A 1,000-word article takes ~5 min to read and ~8 min to speak.

Does WordLab store my text?

No. Everything runs in your browser. No text is ever sent to a server. It's safe for confidential content.

What's the difference between characters with and without spaces?

With spaces counts every character including spaces — this is what most platforms (Twitter, SMS) use. Without spaces counts only non-whitespace characters.
